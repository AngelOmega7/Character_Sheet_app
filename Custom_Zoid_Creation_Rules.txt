CUSTOM ZOID CREATION RULES
Draft baseline for playtesting
==============================================================================

PURPOSE

Players create custom Zoids by modifying an existing chassis benchmark. This
keeps construction costs comparable to existing Zoids while allowing distinct
names, appearances, and combat roles.

These are proposed tabletop rules. Creating this document does not add custom
Zoid creation functionality to the Character Sheet app.

1. CHOOSE A BENCHMARK ZOID
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose an existing Zoid with a similar size, intended combat role, required
level, movement capabilities, and weapon/system loadout.

Start with its unmodified stats, fixed weapons and systems, and removable
equipment slots. A new name or visual redesign is free. Changes to Zoid type
require GM review because type affects ability eligibility.

Use the benchmark's FULL construction cost, including prerequisite Zoids.
An evolved Zoid's listed cost may represent only the final upgrade step.

Examples of cumulative gold costs:

  Shield Liger       38,000g
  Blade Liger        70,000g
  Zaber Fang         40,000g
  Proto Zaber        58,000g
  Great Zaber       108,000g

These totals exclude separately required cores and parts. Supply the cores
and parts required throughout the construction chain as well as the gold.
Do not count a prerequisite twice: a supplied prerequisite Zoid satisfies
that portion of the chain rather than requiring another purchase.

2. PRICE IMPROVEMENTS USING EXISTING UPGRADE RULES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each upgrade point has a flat cost, regardless of the current rank.

  Frame improvement              Cost per point       Maximum
  +30 Speed                         5,000g             5 points
  +1 Reaction                       5,000g             5 points
  +5 Defense                        5,000g             5 points
  +10 Carry capacity                5,000g             5 points

  Fixed-weapon improvement       Cost per point       Maximum
  +20 damage to one weapon         10,000g             5 per weapon
  +1 to hit for one weapon         10,000g             5 per weapon

Damage and to-hit improvements are separate tracks for each fixed weapon.
These weapon upgrade tracks do not apply to removable weapons.

Construction improvements consume the SAME upgrade allowance as improvements
purchased later. A weapon built with three damage points can receive only two
more damage points, not another five.

Record these improvements separately from the benchmark's base stats so they
receive the same calculations as ordinary upgrades.

HEALTH, ARMOR, AND RANGE

For this initial ruleset, retain the benchmark's Health, Armor, and Range.
The app does not have equivalent upgrade tracks for these stats, and Armor
can change combat effectiveness sharply.

For substantially different values, select a closer benchmark or have the GM
price the design as a new chassis tier using comparable existing Zoids.

3. CUSTOMIZE WEAPONS AND SYSTEMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use existing weapon profiles as templates. Include the complete profile:

  - Damage and accuracy.
  - Target count and area of effect.
  - Range restrictions.
  - Heat, recharge, and activation rules.
  - Damage tags and special effects.

A rename or visual redesign is free. Changing tags is not automatically
cosmetic: Electrical, Acid, Homing, Piercing, and similar tags can change a
weapon's effectiveness and interactions with abilities.

Initial design limits:

  - Up to four fixed weapons or systems.
  - Retain the benchmark's removable equipment slot layout.
  - A fixed shield, booster, or other system occupies a fixed slot.
  - Removable equipment retains its normal weight and restrictions.

For a mechanically different fixed weapon, compare against a Zoid that
already carries something similar. Equipment Guide prices can inform the
adjustment, but a removable weapon's price is not automatically a fair price
for the same weapon as a fixed system.

Price multi-target attacks by their actual effect, not just listed damage.
Dealing 100 damage to each of four targets is substantially different from
splitting 100 total damage between four targets.

New fixed weapons, special systems, and changes to their restrictions require
a GM-approved price adjustment. This baseline does not assign them universal
point costs.

4. ALLOW LIMITED TRADE-OFFS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suggested starting rule:

  Reducing a stat grants half the corresponding improvement cost as design
  credit. Total credit cannot exceed 20% of the benchmark's cumulative gold
  cost, excluding separately supplied cores and parts.

Examples:

  -30 Speed                         2,500g credit
  -5 Defense                        2,500g credit
  -20 damage on a retained weapon   5,000g credit

Only meaningful reductions qualify. Removing damage from a weapon that will
be discarded does not provide credit toward other improvements. The GM must
approve reductions that would have little practical effect on the design.

Credits are construction discounts, not gold paid to the player. They do not
remove core or part requirements. This discount rule is separate from the
app's normal refund for removing a purchased upgrade point.

5. COMPARE THE DESIGN AGAINST THREE EXISTING ZOIDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before approving a design, compare it with three existing Zoids around its
intended level. Use the same comparison conditions: unmodified chassis and
listed systems, with purchased upgrades identified separately and no pilot
or Organoid bonuses.

Evaluate these categories separately:

  SURVIVABILITY
  Health, Armor, Defense, shields, and defensive systems.

  MOBILITY
  Speed, Reaction, and movement capabilities.

  OFFENSE
  Accuracy, strongest repeatable attack, multi-target damage, heat, recharge,
  and special weapon effects.

  UTILITY
  Range, carry capacity, equipment slots, and special systems.

If the custom Zoid is at least as good in every category and better in one,
it should not cost less than that comparator's full construction cost. Include
material requirements in the comparison rather than comparing gold alone.

The custom Zoid's required level must be at least the benchmark's required
level. A substantially stronger design should move into the level band of
its stronger comparators.

6. CALCULATE THE CONSTRUCTION PRICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Benchmark cumulative gold cost
  + Purchased upgrade points
  + GM-priced weapon and system changes
  - Approved design credits
  = Proposed construction price

Then apply the comparison check in Section 5 and adjust the price or design
if necessary. Required cores and parts are supplied separately.

7. WORKED EXAMPLE: CUSTOM SHIELD LIGER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retain Shield Liger's existing weapons, systems, and equipment slots. Add:

  Shield Liger benchmark                    38,000g
  +60 Speed: two frame points               10,000g
  +5 Defense: one frame point                5,000g
  +20 Laser Saber damage: one weapon point  10,000g
                                           -------
  Total                                    63,000g + Zoid Core H1

Relevant base-plus-upgrade values:

  Speed                 310
  Defense                18
  Laser Saber damage    170

Record two Speed points, one Defense point, and one Laser Saber damage point
as already used. These count toward their respective five-point limits.

Compare this design with Blade Liger at 70,000g plus the materials required
by its construction chain. Blade Liger still has higher Health, Defense,
Range, carry capacity, and a stronger main melee weapon. The custom design
therefore has a plausible price rather than simply replacing Blade Liger.

This is an illustrative comparison; a final design still needs the full
three-Zoid review, including its required level.

8. PLAYTESTING AND FUTURE EXPANSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Start with this benchmark-and-modify system and playtest several builds.
Track whether custom Zoids consistently outperform similarly priced existing
Zoids, and whether any trade-offs provide discounts without meaningful costs.

Use those results to calibrate future independent pricing for Health, Armor,
Range, new fixed systems, movement capabilities, and equipment-slot changes.
