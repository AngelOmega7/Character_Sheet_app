REMOVABLE EQUIPMENT - STAT BLOCK COMPANION
==============================================================================
91 catalog weapons; 7 Zoid-specific removable weapon entries.
61 catalog assist items; 4 Zoid-specific assist entries.

Gravity Cannon is unpriced by design.
These prices belong to this companion document, not the app's catalog.

HOW TO USE THIS REFERENCE
This companion lists removable equipment from the Character Sheet app. Section
1 covers weapons; Section 2 covers assist equipment. Each section lists
catalog items alphabetically, followed by Zoid-specific entries. Separate Zoid
versions retain their individual stats and source restrictions, even when
names match.
Values are catalog stats before pilot bonuses, upgrades, Organoids, or combat
conditions. Missing values mean not listed, not zero. Heat and other optional
modifiers appear wherever recorded. Effects preserve the catalog wording,
including descriptions embedded in item names.
Assist equipment includes armor, shields, sensors, boosters, and other support
systems. Catalog assist items can use Assist or Weapon and Assist slots.
Zoid-specific entries retain their source slot types and restrictions.
