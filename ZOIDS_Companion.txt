ZOIDS - STAT BLOCK COMPANION
==============================================================================
154 Zoids: 100 base entries and 54 CAU forms; plus 4 transport Zoids.

HOW TO USE THIS REFERENCE
Entries use the current Character Sheet app reference data. Base Zoids are
alphabetical, with their CAU forms immediately following. Each form has a
complete, independent stat block. Transport Zoids appear in an appendix.
Stats are catalog values before pilot bonuses, upgrades, Organoids, equipment
modifiers, or combat conditions. Active/passive system bonuses are listed
separately. A missing value means not listed in the catalog; explicit zeroes
are retained. Heat is shown wherever explicitly recorded.
Weapon effects and source damage notes preserve the catalog wording. Build
requirements are listed where available.
